## 🎬 4-Minute Live Demo Flow

### Act 1: The Problem & The Passport (60 seconds)
1. **Navigate to:** `/inventory`
   - Point out the registered datasets.
   - Click on **DEMO – Customer Records**.
2. **Examine the Data Passport:**
   - Show field classifications (`email`, `full_name`, `date_of_birth`).
   - Highlight the cryptographic passport hash proving tamper-resistance.
   - **Key Narrative:** *"In modern AI workflows, teams frequently merge demographic records with financial histories. Individually, each dataset may seem benign, but together they introduce compound privacy risks."*

### Act 2: Combination & Linkage Risk (60 seconds)
1. **Navigate to:** `/combination`
2. **Select both demo datasets:**
   - Select `DEMO – Customer Records` + `DEMO – Customer Purchase History`.
   - Click **Analyze Combination**.
3. **Show results:**
   - Highlight the quasi-identifier intersection: `customer_id`, `zip_code`.
   - Point to the dynamic risk escalation from Tier 2 to Tier 4 (high re-identification danger).

### Act 3: Lineage Graph & Cycle Protection (45 seconds)
1. **Navigate to:** `/lineage`
2. **Inspect the DAG:**
   - Observe the transformation edge: `Customer Records` ──[COMBINATION]──▶ `Customer Purchase History`.
   - Explain how DataTether automatically validates graph acyclicity to avoid infinite recursive loops.

### Act 4: The 5-Stage Investigation & Audit Decision (75 seconds)
1. **Navigate to:** `/investigation`
2. **Configure proposed usage:**
   - Select both demo datasets.
   - Select proposed purpose: **AI Training**.
   - Click **Run Investigation**.
3. **Present the 4 Evidence Quadrants:**
   - **Quadrant 1 (Observed Facts):** Shows the combined schema and origins.
   - **Quadrant 2 (Privacy Risk):** Explains quasi-identifier linkage score.
   - **Quadrant 3 (Policy Constraints):** Flags that AI Training is blocked without prior tokenization and explicit customer consent.
   - **Quadrant 4 (AI Policy Explainer):** Shows plain-English synthesis generated by Llama / fallback rules.
4. **Governance Decision:**
   - Point to `overall_status`: **BLOCKED** (policy constraint hit).
   - Point to `human_review_status`: **PENDING_DPO_APPROVAL** (distinguishing automated check from human discretion).
   - Click **Approve with Conditions** or **Reject** to demonstrate the Data Protection Officer (DPO) governance audit trail.
